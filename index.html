Risk Assessment

🏛️
Tenure & Title Risk
Freehold Chanote title — the strongest land tenure available in Thailand. No leasehold expiry, no renewal risk, no conversion uncertainty.
LOW
🏷️
Brand & Franchise Risk
Radisson RED is a growing lifestyle brand with strong distribution. Franchise terms need review, but asset can rebrand to alternative IHG, Marriott, or independent flag if needed.
LOW
📉
Revenue Ramp Risk
Post-Dec 2023 renovation — property is still stabilising. Current ADR THB 3,200 vs projected THB 4,587 represents a 43% uplift that requires active revenue management and brand leverage.
MEDIUM
🌏
Tourism Demand Risk
Phuket welcomes 14.5M+ tourists annually. Source market diversification (Russia, India, Europe) mitigates over-reliance on any single corridor. Thailand's geopolitical neutrality is a structural advantage.
LOW
🏗️
Supply Pipeline Risk
41 new hotel projects in Phuket pipeline 2026–2030. Patong remains supply-constrained due to limited freehold land availability — this asset's scale (390 keys) is a defensive moat.
MEDIUM
💱
Foreign Exchange Risk
THB revenues for foreign investors carry exchange exposure. However, Phuket's USD-correlated pricing (international travellers) provides a natural partial hedge for FX-sensitive buyers.
LOW
⚖️
Regulatory / Zoning Risk
Special Commercial Zone zoning is the most permissive category for Patong. Mixed-use hospitality, retail, and entertainment uses are fully permitted. BOI incentives may apply for major CAPEX programmes.
LOW
🔧
CAPEX & Operational Risk
Dec 2023 renovation reduces near-term CAPEX requirements. However, a rebrand scenario (Scenario C) requires THB 400M+ CAPEX commitment, requiring experienced project management and brand standard execution.
MEDIUM
